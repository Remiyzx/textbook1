Greetings & Names

Pg 4
# Pinyin English Collocation & Example
1 nǐ you
Nǐ hǎo. (Hello.)
2 hǎo good, well, fine, OK
hěn hǎo (very good)
Wǒ hěn hǎo. (I am very good.)
3 Lù Yǔpíng name of a Chinese reporter
Lù Yǔpíng, nǐ hǎo. (Lu Yuping, hello.)
4 Lìbō name of a Canadian student
Lìbō, nǐ hǎo. (Libo, hello.)

💡 Third-tone Sandhi

Written: nǐ hǎo
➔
Spoken: ní hǎo

* Note: You can call someone's name first before saying 'Nǐ hǎo', or say 'Nǐ hǎo' first then call their name.

Well-being & Particles

Pg 5
# Pinyin English Collocation & Example
1 ma particle for Yes/No Question
Nǐ hǎo ma? (How are you?)
2 wǒ I, me
Wǒ hěn hǎo. (I am very well.)
3 hěn very
Wǒ hěn hǎo. (I am very well.)
4 ne particle for Bounce-back Question
Nǐ ne? (And you?)
5 yě too, also
yě hěn hǎo (also very well)
Wǒ yě hěn hǎo. (I am also very well.)
6 Lín Nà name of a British student
Lín Nà, nǐ hǎo. (Lin Na, hello.)

Structures

S + hěn + Adj

Wǒ hěn hǎo. (I am very well.)

S + yě + hěn + Adj

Wǒ yě hěn hǎo. (I am also very well.)

S + yě + V + O

Wǒ yě qù diànyǐngyuàn. (I also go to the cinema.)

Particles

ma Yes/No Question

Nǐ hǎo ma?

ne Bounce-back Question

Nǐ ne?
