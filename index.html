Grammar Focus

Sentence Structure

Subject + adv (hěn/yě) + adjective

The adverb hěn (very)

  • • Placed before the adjective.

Wǒ hěn hǎo.

The adverb yě (also, too)

  • • Placed before other adverbs and adjectives.

Wǒ yě hěn hǎo.

Particles Comparison

ma Yes/No Question

Nǐ hǎo ma?

ne Bounce-back Question

Nǐ ne?
