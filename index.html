Greetings & Names

# Pinyin English Phrase / Example

Lìbō, nǐ hǎo.

Well-being & Particles

Pg 5
# Pinyin English Phrase / Example
1 ma particle for yes-no question
Nǐ hǎo ma?
2 wǒ I, me
Wǒ hěn hǎo.
3 hěn very
Wǒ hěn hǎo.
4 ne particle for bounce-back questions
Nǐ ne?
5 yě too, also
Wǒ yě hěn hǎo.
6 Lín Nà name of a British student
Lín Nà, nǐ hǎo.

Structure

1. S + hěn + Adj
2. S + yě + hěn + Adj

Particles

ma Yes/no Question
ne Bounce-back Q.
