Word Order & Adverb "hěn"

Subject (S) + Adverb (hěn) + Adjective (A)

Wǒ + hěn + hǎo.

Note: Even when you are not "very" good, Chinese sentence structure prefers adding hěn in front of adjectives like hǎo.

The Adverb "yě"

The adverb yě goes BEFORE the whole situation (adverb + adjective). It never goes inside or at the end.

Wǒ +
yě +
hěn hǎo.

Particles: "ma" & "ne"

ma Yes/No Question

Always placed at the very end of the sentence.

Nǐ hǎo ma?

ne Bounce-back Question

Used for elliptical "And you?" questions.

Nǐ ne?
