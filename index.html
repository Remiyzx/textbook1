3 hěn very

4 ne Bounce-back Question particle
nǐ ne

5 yě too, also
yě hěn hǎo
Wǒ yě hěn hǎo.
6 Lín Nà name of a British student

Lín Nà hěn hǎo.

Sentence Structures

Structure 1 Subject + hěn + Adjective

Wǒ hěn hǎo.

I am very good / I am fine.

Structure 2 Subject + yě + hěn + Adjective

Wǒ yě hěn hǎo.

I am also fine.

Structure 3 Subject + yě + Verb + Object

Wǒ yě qù diànyǐngyuàn.

I go to the cinema too.

💬 Focus: Greetings and asking about well-being

This text introduces the Yes/No Question particle "ma" and the Bounce-back Question particle "ne". Click the English translations below to reveal the Pinyin dialogue!

▼

2. Lin Na:

I'm fine, and you?

▼

I'm fine too.

▼

Yě hěn hǎo.

Core Grammar Summary

👋 Greetings

Nǐ hǎo. (Hello.)

Nǐ hǎo ma? (How are you?)

🧱 Structures

1. S + hěn + Adj

2. S + yě + hěn + Adj

3. S + yě + V + O

❓ Particles

1. Yes/No Question: ma

2. Bounce-back Question: ne
